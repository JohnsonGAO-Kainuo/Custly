# Custly CRM — 3‑Minute Pitch Script (Final)

**Hello everyone, I’m Johnson Gao. Today I’ll present Custly CRM, a lightweight CRM for SMEs.** 【Slide 1】

**Let me briefly summarize what Custly does.** 【Slide 2】  
Custly centralizes **Contacts, Companies, Deals, and Tasks**,
so small teams can get started quickly without heavy training.

**Now here are the key pain points we target.** 【Slide 3】  
Customer data is scattered, traditional CRMs are heavy and expensive,
and local teams need localization in daily workflows.  
Custly is designed to be faster to adopt, more affordable, and more industry‑adaptable.

**So how does it work in practice?** 【Slide 4】  
The workflow is simple: **Leads → Qualify → Convert → Retain**.  
We keep the core CRM actions clear and lightweight.

**With that overview, let’s turn to the live demo so you can see it directly.** 【Demo】
