# Slide 2 — Project Summary (30–40 words)

Custly is a calm, modern CRM for SMEs that centralizes customer data, manages deals and tasks, and offers industry templates with EN/简体/繁體 support—enabling privacy‑first deployments and fast onboarding from lead to revenue.

---

# Slide 3 — Problem & Market Need

**Problems**
- Customer context scattered across chat, email, spreadsheets.
- Traditional CRMs are expensive, heavy, slow to adopt.
- Local teams need bilingual workflows and industry presets.
- Privacy concerns limit cloud‑only SaaS adoption.

**Needs**
- Fast setup without long implementation cycles.
- Affordable pricing with clear ROI for SMEs.
- Localized UX (EN / 简体 / 繁體).
- Flexible deployment: hosted or self‑hosted data.

---

# Slide 4 — Solution Overview (How It Works)

**Core workflow**
1) Capture leads → import/create contacts
2) Qualify → notes + activity timeline
3) Convert → deal pipeline + tasks
4) Retain → follow‑ups, tags, reports

**What makes Custly different**
- Template‑first CRM (e‑commerce + counseling presets)
- Privacy‑first backend (PocketBase)
- Calm UI for daily focus
